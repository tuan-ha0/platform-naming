    Talkiatry Introduces , the Technology and Data Platform Behind Its Psychiatric Care

    brings the company's electronic health record, patient matching, AI documentation and outcomes reporting together under one name

    NEW YORK (launch date to come). Talkiatry, a national provider of in-network psychiatric care, today introduced , the technology and data platform its clinicians use to deliver care. combines the company's purpose-built electronic health record with tools for patient matching, clinical documentation, care intelligence and payer reporting.

    Talkiatry built in-house because general-purpose medical software was not designed for psychiatry. The platform follows how psychiatrists work: longer visits, medication management over months and years, and close coordination with therapists and primary care.

    “We started Talkiatry so people could get high-quality psychiatric care with their insurance. is how we keep doing that as we grow. It gives our clinicians better tools and gives our partners a clearer view of outcomes.”

    Robert Krayn, co-founder and CEO of Talkiatry
